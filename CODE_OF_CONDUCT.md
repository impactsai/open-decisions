# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and open to correction. Critique proposals and evidence,
not a contributor's identity, background, or motives. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Disclose material conflicts of interest. Do not claim endorsement, credentials,
or implementation results you do not have. Give reviewers enough information to
reproduce technical claims without exposing private data.

Repository maintainers may remove abusive material, close disruptive discussions,
or restrict participation, with a proportionate explanation where doing so does
not reveal private information. Contributors may request reconsideration by a
maintainer who was not directly involved. The future community charter should
establish a named, confidential reporting route; none is represented as operating
yet. Do not put sensitive incident details into a public issue.
