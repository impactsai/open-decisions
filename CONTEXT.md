# Comparison decisions

Language for the comparison profile of the Open Decisions community draft.

## Language

**Comparison kit**: A versioned definition of the evidence, assessment methods,
constraints, utilities, permitted preferences, and policy used to compare options.

**Assessment**: An evaluator's observation or distribution over a criterion's
possible values, tied to an option, evidence, and an evaluation method.
_Avoid_: decision, confidence score.

**Evaluation context**: The frozen kit, options, and evidence against which
assessments are produced. It excludes a particular user's preference weights.

**Utility**: The value assigned to an assessed outcome under an explicit criterion
scale. Utility and likelihood are different quantities.

**Preference scenario**: One admissible set of criterion weights and its declared
share of the preference uncertainty model.

**Joint scenario**: A combination of assessment outcomes and preference weights
with a declared mass, preserving dependence between those quantities.

**Rank support**: The share of modeled scenarios in which an option receives a
particular rank. It is conditional on the evidence and declared uncertainty model.
_Avoid_: probability that the choice is objectively correct.

**Recommendation**: A policy determination made from a comparison result. It
does not authorize an action or attest to the truth of the underlying evidence.
